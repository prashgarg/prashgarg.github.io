# Paper figure pilot provenance

These two assets are crops of original figures from the authors' primary
source PDFs. They are not redrawn or re-plotted.

## Global Automation Atlas

- Asset: `public/images/papers/global-automation-atlas-figure-1.png`
- Source: Figure 1, [Global Automation Atlas](https://automationatlas.org/downloads/automation-atlas-paper.pdf), May 2026 version.
- PDF page: 5.
- Crop: figure title, panels (a) and (b), and axes/legend; surrounding body text and the paper's note were removed.
- Visible source values: 124 country shares constructed from 2,330,776 task-country labels; exposed task share against log GDP per capita.
- Public companion source: [automationatlas.org/paper](https://automationatlas.org/paper/).

## Causal Claims in Economics

- Asset: `public/images/papers/causal-claims-figure-2.png`
- Source: Figure 2, [Causal Claims in Economics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.06873), revised February 2026 version.
- PDF page: 16.
- Crop: figure title and panels (a) and (b); the explanatory note and surrounding body text were removed.
- Visible source values: average proportion of causal edges per paper over 1980–2023, plus pre-2000/post-2000 field comparisons.
- Public companion source: [causal.claims](https://www.causal.claims/).
